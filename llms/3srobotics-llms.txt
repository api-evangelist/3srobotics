# 3S robotics

> 3S robotics (Shanghai Shengshi Weisheng Technology Co., Ltd. / 上海昇视唯盛科技有限公司) is a
> Chinese intelligent-robotics manufacturer founded in 2020, headquartered in the Songjiang
> district of Shanghai, with a smart factory in the Haining Economic Development Zone, Zhejiang.
> It is a member company of the listed Harbin Institute of Technology intelligent-robotics group
> (哈工智能), and its subsidiary Hagong Hyundai (哈工现代, HGXD) builds general-purpose industrial
> robots. 3S robotics develops an in-house "AI + 3D + ROBOT" stack — machine-vision cameras,
> motion control and offline path-planning software — packaged as task-specific intelligent
> robots for welding, grinding and assembly that need no teach-pendant programming. Its flagship
> line is a 3D-vision intelligent welding robot for steel-structure, tunnel arch, rail-transit
> and heavy-equipment fabrication.

## Status

3S robotics publishes **no public API**, no developer portal, no SDK and no machine-readable
interface contract. The software it sells is embedded machine-side vision and motion-control
tooling shipped with the robot, not a network service.
